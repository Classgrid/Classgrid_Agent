# Profile (phone app)

Your profile is who you are on Classgrid: photo, name, age, role, bio, hobbies, social links, your WhatsApp number
and how you sign in. Change any of it from the phone.

## How to open your profile
- Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap your photo and name at the bottom, or
- Open **Settings** (the gear at the bottom of the side menu) and tap **Profile**.

The top card shows your photo, name, email and "Member since" month and year. Pull down to refresh.

## How to change your profile photo
1. Open **Profile** and tap **Edit Photo** under your picture.
2. Pick a picture from your phone.
3. Drag to move it and pinch to zoom inside the round frame.
4. Tap **Save** ("Profile photo updated"), or **Cancel**.

## How to change your name or another detail
1. Open **Profile**. Under **Basic Information** tap the row: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Age**, **Bio** or
   **Hobbies**.
2. Type the new value in the sheet and tap **Save** ("Saved").
- **Date of Birth** opens a calendar: pick the day and tap OK. Your age is worked out from it.
- **Role** opens a list (Student, Teacher, Software Engineer and many more): tap one and it saves at once.
- The first name cannot be empty.

## How to add social links and tech stack
1. Open **Profile** and scroll to **Social Status**.
2. Tap **Tech Stack**, **LinkedIn**, **GitHub**, **Twitter / X**, **Coding Profile (LeetCode/HackerRank)**,
   **Portfolio Website**, **Instagram** or **Facebook**.
3. Type the full link starting with https:// and tap **Save**. A saved link shows an open-link icon to visit it.

## How to change your WhatsApp number
1. Open **Profile**. Under **Basic Information** tap **WhatsApp Number** (shown with **Verified** once you have one).
2. Type the number with the country code (for example 919876543210) and tap **Send code**.
3. Type the 6-digit code from WhatsApp and tap **Verify** ("WhatsApp number verified"). **Send a new code** sends
   another one.
- No number yet: the row is not in Profile. Use **Settings** > **Verify WhatsApp number** instead.

## How to change or set your password
1. Open **Profile** and scroll to **Sign-in**. It lists how you sign in (**Email and password**, **Google**,
   **GitHub**) and which one you used last.
2. Tap **Change password**, fill **Current password**, **New password** (at least 8 characters) and **Confirm new
   password**, then tap **Update password**.
   - Forgot it: tap **Forgot your password? Email me a link** in the same sheet.
3. An account without a password (Google or GitHub only) shows **Set a password**: tap it and a link to set one is
   emailed to you (valid for 10 minutes).

## Token activity
Profile also shows **Token activity**: a chart of how much you used the AI, with **Daily**, **Weekly** and
**Cumulative** views. Tap a square to see that day's number.

## Good to know
- Changes are saved to your account and show on the website and desktop app too.
- To hide your email, hobbies or bio from other people, use **Settings** > **Privacy**.
- Extra email addresses for the same account are added in **Settings** > **Emails**, not in Profile.

## Not on the phone
- The profile banner is not in the phone app. Change it on chat.classgrid.in: account menu (your name at the bottom
  of the sidebar) > **Profile**.
