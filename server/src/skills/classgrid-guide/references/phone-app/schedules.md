# Schedules and reminders (phone app)

A scheduled task is something Classgrid AI does at a set time, once or on repeat: a reminder, a morning briefing,
a weekly email, a WhatsApp message and so on. Tasks are always created by Classgrid AI in a chat; the Schedule
screen lists them and lets you change or delete them.

## How to create a task by asking the AI
1. In any chat, ask for it, for example "Remind me every Monday at 9 AM to send the report".
2. Classgrid asks for anything missing (what, how often, when), then creates the task.

## How to create a task from the Schedule screen
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap **Schedule**. The **Scheduled tasks** screen opens.
3. Tap **New task** at the bottom right.
4. Type in **Describe a task to schedule** and tap the send arrow. Or tap **Help me set one up**, or an idea under
   **Ideas** or **Integrations** (it fills the box; change the words in brackets, then send).
5. A new chat opens. Answer Classgrid's questions; it creates the task.

## How to see your tasks
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰), then tap **Schedule**.
2. Each card shows the status, the title, the next run (for example "Next: Tomorrow at 9:00 AM") and how it repeats.
3. To filter, tap the filter icon at the top right and pick **All tasks**, **Pending**, **Completed**, **Failed** or
   **Cancelled**.
4. Tap a task to open its page.

## What a task's page shows
- The status and the title.
- **Schedule**: when it runs (**Next run**, **Scheduled for** or **Was scheduled for**), **Repeats**, the **Email**
  subject or **WhatsApp** message when it sends one, and the **Task ID** (tap to copy).
- **Summary**, **Details** and **Notes** when the task has them.
- **Timeline** (the run history): **Created**, **Scheduled**, each **Rescheduled**, how many times it ran (for example
  **Ran 3 times**), then **Completed**, **Failed** (with the reason) or **Cancelled**, or the next run.

## How to change the date or time
1. Open the task's page (side menu, **Schedule**, tap the task).
2. Under **Reschedule** (pending tasks) or **Try again at a new time** (failed tasks), tap **Date** and **Time**.
3. Tap **Update schedule** (or **Reschedule task** for a failed one). The time must be in the future.

## How to edit what a task does
1. Open the task's page and tap **Edit with Classgrid** (or tap ⋮ at the top right, then **Edit with Classgrid**).
   On the list you can also long-press the task card.
2. Type what should change in **What should change?** and send it.
3. A new chat opens and Classgrid changes the task for you.

## How to delete a task
1. Open the task's page and tap **Delete task** (or tap ⋮, then **Delete**). On the list, long-press the card and tap
   **Delete**.
2. Tap **Delete** in **Delete task?**. Classgrid cancels the task and deletes it. This cannot be undone.

## Good to know
- The task menu also has **Copy link** and **Share** (sends the task's link).
- A link to a task (from **Copy link** or **Share**) opens its page in the app.
- A repeating task runs until its end date (when it has one, shown under **Repeats**) or until you delete it.
- There is no Pause button on the phone. To stop a task, delete it; to change it, use **Edit with Classgrid**.
