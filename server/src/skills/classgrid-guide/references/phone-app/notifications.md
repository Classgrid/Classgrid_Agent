# Notifications (phone app)

The Classgrid phone app can send push notifications to your phone (for example when an answer is ready) and
Classgrid can also email you. You choose which ones you get in Settings > Notifications.

## How to open notification settings
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap the gear (**Settings**).
2. Tap **Notifications**.

## How to turn all push notifications on or off
1. Open **Settings** > **Notifications**.
2. Flip **Enable Push Alerts** ("Receive real-time system notifications").
When it is off, every switch under it is greyed out and no push notifications are sent.

## How to choose what you are notified about
1. Open **Settings** > **Notifications** and make sure **Enable Push Alerts** is on.
2. Under **Notify me about**, flip each switch:
   - **Answers**: when the agent finishes an answer you have not seen.
   - **Images**: when a picture you asked for is ready.
   - **Schedules**: when a scheduled task runs.
   - **Calls**: when a call's summary is ready.
   - **Shared chats**: new messages in chats shared with you.
   - **Product updates**: news about Classgrid.
   - **Remote PC**: when your computer needs your OK for a step.
Each change saves at once.

## How to turn email notifications on or off
1. Open **Settings** > **Notifications**.
2. Under **Email**, flip **Email notifications**.

## If notifications are blocked on the phone
If Android does not let Classgrid show notifications, the Notifications page shows "Notifications are off for
Classgrid on this phone." with a **Turn on** button.
1. Tap **Turn on**.
2. Allow notifications in the window Android shows (or in Android's notification settings for Classgrid, which
   open when the app cannot ask), then come back to the app.

## Good to know
- On Android 13 and newer the app asks once, after your first sign-in, if it may send notifications.
- Tapping a notification opens its chat, schedule or call.
- While the app is open you still get a notification, unless you are already looking at that chat, schedule or call.
- Notifications on this phone are for the account in use. Logging out stops them on this phone.
- These settings are saved on your account, not only on this phone.

## Haptic feedback (the light tap)
The light vibration when you send a message is not a notification setting. It is **Settings** > **Haptic feedback**
(a switch, on by default). It also ticks when you copy something, open a long-press menu, flip a switch or press
Stop. Android's own "Touch feedback" setting also applies.

## Not on the phone
- The notification inbox (**Inbox** and **Archive**) is on chat.classgrid.in: the bell next to your name at the
  bottom of the sidebar.
