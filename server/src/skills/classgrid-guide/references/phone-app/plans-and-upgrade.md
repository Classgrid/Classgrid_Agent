# Plans and upgrade (phone app)

Where a phone user sees which plan they are on, buys a higher plan through Google Play, switches between monthly and
yearly, moves to a lower plan or cancels. Plans bought in the phone app are Google Play subscriptions.

## How to see your current plan
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap the gear icon (**Settings**) at the bottom of the side menu.
3. Tap **Plan**.
4. The card at the top shows the plan name with **Subscribed** or **Free**. The line under it says how the plan is paid:
   - a Google Play plan: **Google Play** with the date it renews (or the date it ends, if it was cancelled);
   - a plan bought on the website: the date it is paid until and **does not renew by itself**.
5. Under **What <plan> gives you** is the list of what the plan includes. Under **Past the plan** are **Credits** (or
   **Top-up credits** on Free) and **Usage**.

The plan name also shows on the Settings page next to your email, and under the greeting on a new chat
(for example **Free plan · Upgrade**).

## How to upgrade (buy a plan)
1. Open the upgrade page. Any of these opens it:
   - Settings, **Plan**, then **Upgrade**;
   - Settings, **Billing**, then **Upgrade** on the **Current plan** card;
   - on a new chat, tap **Upgrade** under the greeting.
2. At the top, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**. A small chip on **Yearly** shows how much you save.
3. Each plan shows its price from Google Play and what it includes. Tap **Upgrade to <plan>** under the plan you want.
4. Google Play's payment sheet opens. Pay there with your Google account.
5. When it is done the app says **You're on <plan> now.**

## How to switch between monthly and yearly (Google Play plan)
1. Open the upgrade page (Settings, **Plan**, **Upgrade**).
2. Tap the other term at the top (**Monthly** or **Yearly**).
3. Your current plan now shows **Switch to <plan>**. Tap it and confirm in Google Play.
- Monthly to yearly starts now.
- Yearly to monthly starts at your next renewal. The app says **Monthly billing starts at your next renewal.**

## How to move to a lower plan
1. Open the upgrade page.
2. Under the lower plan, tap **Switch to <plan>**.
3. For a Google Play plan this opens Google Play's subscriptions page: change the plan there. Google changes it at
   the next renewal.
4. For a plan bought another way, the app says **Your <plan> plan stays until its end date.**

## How to cancel or manage a Google Play plan
1. Open Settings, then **Plan**.
2. Tap **Manage subscription**. It opens Google Play's own page for your Classgrid subscription
   (https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).
3. There you can cancel, or change the payment method.
- After you cancel, the plan keeps working until its end date. The Plan card then says the plan **ends** on that date,
  and Billing says **Cancelled on Google Play, ends on its date**.
- **Manage subscription** shows only for a plan bought through Google Play.

## Good to know
- Plans auto-renew through Google Play until cancelled (the upgrade page says **Subscription auto-renews until
  cancelled**).
- Moving up to a higher plan starts at once. Google takes into account the money or time left on the old plan.
- **Payment pending. Your plan starts once Google Play confirms it.** means Google is still waiting for the payment.
  The plan starts by itself when it goes through.
- **Payment received. It will show here shortly.** means Google took the payment and Classgrid is still confirming it.
  It is sent again by itself; pull down on the page to refresh.
- **Your plan was bought with another Google account. Change it there.** means the plan must be changed from the
  Google account that bought it.
- **Your Google Play subscription is paused or its payment was declined.** means the plan is on hold: tap
  **Manage subscription** under it and fix the payment on Google Play.
- **Google Play is not reachable. Pull down to try again.** means prices could not load: check the connection and pull
  down.
- There is no "Restore purchases" button. The app checks your Google Play purchases by itself when it opens, when you
  come back to it and when you open Plan, Billing or the upgrade page. Pull down on any of them to check again.
- Tapping a locked model in the model picker shows **Upgrade to use <plan>**; tap **Upgrade** to open the upgrade page.
- On the top plan there is no **Upgrade** button. The current plan shows **Current plan**.

## Plan bought on the website
- A plan bought on the Classgrid website (dashboard) cannot be changed in the phone app. Tapping an Upgrade button
  shows: **You can't change your plan in this app, because you bought it on the Classgrid dashboard.** Change it at
  https://chat.classgrid.in under **Settings > Billing**.
- A website plan does not renew by itself. Credits can still be bought in the phone app (see the Billing page).

## Student price
- Apply on the phone: Settings, **Billing**, then **Apply for student prices** on the **Current plan** card.
- The phone app sells only the regular prices. Once approved, the student plan is bought on the pricing page:
  https://classgrid.in/pricing. That purchase is for one month or one year and does not renew by itself.

## Not on the phone
- Buying at the student price: https://classgrid.in/pricing.
- Changing or renewing a plan bought on the website: https://chat.classgrid.in, **Settings > Billing**.
