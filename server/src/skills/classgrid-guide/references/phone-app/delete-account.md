# Delete your account (phone app)

You can delete your Classgrid account straight from the phone app. This permanently removes the account and all its
data (chats, files and projects). It cannot be undone.

## How to delete your account (account with a password)
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap the gear (**Settings**).
2. Scroll to the very bottom and tap **Delete Account** (in red).
3. The **Delete Account** sheet opens: "Permanently delete your account and all associated data." and the warning
   "This action is irreversible. All your data will be permanently lost and cannot be recovered."
4. Under **Type your password to finalize**, type your Classgrid password.
5. Tap the red **Delete Account** button.

## How to delete your account (no password: Google, GitHub or email code sign-in)
If your account has no password of its own, the sheet asks for an emailed code instead.
1. Open **Settings**, scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**.
2. Tap **Send code**. A 6-digit code is emailed to your account's email ("We sent a 6-digit code to your email.").
   **Send a new code** sends another one.
3. Type the code under **Type the 6-digit code from your email to finalize**.
4. Tap the red **Delete Account** button.

## What happens after
- Deleting can take a little while. Keep the app open until it finishes.
- "Account deleted successfully." shows, and the phone signs out of that account.
- If other accounts are signed in on this phone, the app switches to the next one; otherwise the sign-in screen
  shows.
- Your chats, files and projects are deleted with the account. They cannot be brought back.

## Good to know
- Deleting the account does not cancel a plan bought through Google Play. Cancel it first: **Settings** > **Plan** >
  **Manage subscription**, or in Google Play at https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
- To stop without deleting, tap **Cancel**.
- "Incorrect password." means the password was wrong. Try again, or reset it first: **Settings** > **Profile** >
  **Change password** > **Forgot your password? Email me a link**.
- The red button stays greyed out until the password (8 characters or more) or the full 6-digit code is typed.
- Each code is valid for 10 minutes.
- If you only want to stop using Classgrid on this phone, use **Log out** instead (just above **Delete Account**).
