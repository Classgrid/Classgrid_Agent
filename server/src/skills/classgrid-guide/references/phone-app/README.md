# Classgrid phone app (Android): guide pages

These pages say how to do things in the Classgrid app on an Android phone, with the exact buttons the app shows. Read
the page that matches the question with read_app_guide, page "phone/<name>" (for example "phone/accounts"), and give
only the steps it has. Prices, limits and the user's own usage are not in these pages: use get_plans_and_usage.

## Start and account
- getting-started: what the phone app is, the first open and sign-up steps, a tour of the chat screen and the side menu.
- sign-in: every way to sign in (Google, GitHub, email and password, emailed code), codes, errors, Forgot password, Log out.
- accounts: add another account, switch between accounts (up to 3 on a phone), sign one out.
- profile: open and edit the profile: photo, name and details, role, links, WhatsApp number, password.
- settings: a map of every Settings row, what it does and which page covers it.
- notifications: push alerts and what they notify about, email notifications, the light tap on send.
- privacy-and-data: privacy switches, incognito chats, deleting chats, Archived chats, deleting saved offline data.
- delete-account: delete the account from the phone and what happens after.
- devices: see signed-in devices, log one out or all of them, the device limit.

## Chatting
- chat-basics: new chat, incognito, send, stop, edit and resend, copy, Helpful or Not helpful, retry, reply, read aloud.
- message-box: the message box and its + sheet (camera, photos, files, library, create image, sketch, web search, deep research, plugins, AI Hub), @ apps, pasted text cards, dictation.
- models: choosing a model and how hard it thinks (Fast, Normal, Deep), locked models.
- answers: what an answer can hold: steps, code, diagrams, pictures, tables, links, follow-up questions, cards.
- chat-history: the chat list: search, open, rename, pin, archive and unarchive, delete, Add to Home screen, move to a project.
- share-chat: share a chat as a read-only link or with people (Share Grid), and open a shared chat.
- voice-mode: voice mode (start, talk, interrupt, end) and the voice settings.
- images: making pictures with the AI, saving and sharing them, the Images screen.

## Plans, usage and storage
- plans-and-upgrade: see the plan, buy or change a plan through Google Play, cancel, plans bought on the website, student price.
- usage: the Usage screen, when limits refill, what to do when a limit is reached.
- billing: the Billing screen: credits (top-ups), the low credit reminder, student prices, invoices, restoring purchases.
- storage: what uses storage and how to free it.

## Work
- projects: projects: create, open, the Chats, Files and Project tabs, move a chat in, rename, logo, delete.
- library: the Library and its Trash: open, search, upload, share, download, delete, restore.
- docs: Docs: the list and its filter, open, edit, comments, share, delete, the ⋮ menu.
- pages-and-videos: pages and videos the AI makes: the card in the chat, the viewer, video controls, scenes, comments, share, download.
- schedules: scheduled tasks and reminders: make, see, reschedule, edit, delete, run history.
- calls: calling the Classgrid AI on WhatsApp, the Calls list, transcripts and summaries.
- emails: the sign-in email addresses screen, and emailing tasks to the agent.
- skills: skills, built-in skills, how the agent answers, About you (custom instructions).
- grids: Share Grid chats with other people: join, invite, people, leave.

## Apps and computer
- plugins: plugins: open the list, connect, Try in chat, disconnect, add a connector.
- connected-apps: the Connected apps screen: outside apps that use Classgrid, their access, remove access.
- using-apps-in-chat: asking the AI to use a connected app, picking one with @, following and answering its steps.
- remote-pc: Remote PC: follow and answer a chat running on the computer, set-up, the coloured dot, permission cards.
- remote-pc-tools: inside a Remote PC chat: Details, Changes, Review, Files, Terminal, and Remote PC settings.
- troubleshooting: offline, answers that stop, sign-in problems, plugins that will not connect, updates, support.
