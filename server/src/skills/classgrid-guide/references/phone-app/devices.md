# Signed-in devices (phone app)

The Devices page lists every place your Classgrid account is signed in (website, desktop app, phone app, code
editor) and lets you log out any of them, or all at once. Use it if you lost a device or reached your plan's device
limit.

## How to see your signed-in devices
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap the gear (**Settings**).
2. Tap **Devices**.
3. Each device shows its name, its type (**Website**, **Desktop app**, **Phone app** or **Code editor**) and "Last
   used" with the day and time. The phone you are holding is marked **This device**.
- If your plan limits devices, the top line says how many of your allowed devices are in use ("(used) of (allowed)
  devices").
- Pull down to refresh the list.

## How to log out one device
1. Open **Settings** > **Devices**.
2. Tap **Log out** on the device's row.
3. Tap **Log out** in the "Log out (device name)?" box to confirm.
- That device is signed out and has to sign in again to use your account.
- If you pick **This device**, this phone is signed out too and you go to the sign-in screen.

## How to log out from all devices
1. Open **Settings** > **Devices**.
2. Tap **Log out from all devices** (in red, under the list).
3. Tap **Log out from all** to confirm.
- Every device is signed out, this phone too. You go to the sign-in screen and each device has to sign in again.

## How to free a device slot when sign-in is refused
If you try to sign in and your plan's device limit is reached, the sign-in screen shows the limit message and your
devices under it.
1. Tap **Log out** on a device you no longer use.
2. Tap **Log out** to confirm.
3. Sign in again.

## Good to know
- Logging out a device cannot be undone, but the device can simply sign in again.
- To only sign out this phone, you can also use **Settings** > **Log out**.
