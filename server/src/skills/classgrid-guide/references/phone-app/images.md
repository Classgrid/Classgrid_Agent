# Making pictures and the Images screen (phone app)

How to ask Classgrid AI for a picture in the Android app, and how to see, save, share and delete the pictures it made.

## How to make a picture
1. In the chat, tap **+** at the left of the message box.
2. Tap **Create image**. **@Create image** goes into the box.
3. Type what you want, for example "a sunset over the sea", and tap **Send**.
- You can also just ask in your own words, for example "Create an image of a sunset over the sea".
- While the picture is being made, a square with moving dots shows in the answer. Then the picture appears.
- To draw something yourself and send it, tap **+**, then **Sketch** (see the message box page).

## How to open, save or share a picture in the chat
1. Tap the picture in the answer. It opens full screen; pinch to zoom.
2. Tap **Download** at the top to save it on the phone (in Downloads, in a Classgrid folder).
3. Or tap ⋮ for **Download**, **Open with another app**, **Open in browser**, **Copy link** and **Share**.
4. Tap **X** to close.

## How to open the Images screen
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap the gear at the bottom (Settings).
2. Tap **Images**.
- Every picture the AI made for you is there, newest first, grouped by month, two to a row. Pull down to refresh.

## How to view, share or delete a picture on the Images screen
1. Tap a picture to see it full screen. Pinch or double-tap to zoom.
2. At the top: **Share image** (opens the phone's share menu with the picture's link), **Delete Image**, and **Close**.
3. Or long-press a picture for **View**, **Share** and **Delete**.
4. To delete, confirm with **Delete**. It is removed from your history for good and cannot be undone.

## The same pictures from the message box
1. Tap **+**, then **Images** under **AI Hub**.
2. Tap a picture to see it with its date; tap the bin icon (**Delete Image**) to delete it.

## Good to know
- To save a picture to the phone, open it from the chat and tap **Download**. The Images screen shares the link
  but has no download button.
- A picture whose link has stopped working shows **Image Expired** and is removed from the list.
- To be told when a picture you asked for is ready: Settings, **Notifications**, turn on **Images**.
- Making pictures counts toward your plan's limits; your plan decides how many you can make.
