# Getting started (phone app)

The Classgrid Android app is Classgrid AI on your phone: chat with the AI, see your projects, Library, docs,
schedules, calls and Remote PC chats, and change your account settings. This page covers the first open and where
each main thing lives.

## How to open the app for the first time
1. Open **Classgrid** on your phone. The sign-in screen shows the Classgrid logo.
2. Tap **Continue with Google**, **Continue with GitHub**, or type your email in **Email address** and tap
   **Continue**. (See "Sign in and sign out (phone app)" for every step.)
3. A new account then finishes sign-up (next section). An account that is already set up goes straight to the chat.
4. On Android 13 and newer the phone asks once if Classgrid may send notifications. Tap **Allow** to get them.

## How to finish sign-up (new account)
After the email (or Google or GitHub) step, a new account answers three short steps:
1. **Add your WhatsApp number**: first tap **Open WhatsApp** and send the hello message to Classgrid (WhatsApp only
   delivers the code after you message Classgrid once). Back in the app, tap the country code button to change the
   country if needed, type your number in **WhatsApp number** and tap **Send code**. Type the 6-digit code from
   WhatsApp and tap **Continue**.
   - Or tap **Skip for now**. You can verify the number later in Settings (**Verify WhatsApp number**).
   - **Change number** on the code step goes back to fix the number.
2. **How old are you?**: type your age and tap **Continue**. You must be 18 or older.
3. **What kind of work do you do?**: tap the role closest to you (Student, Teacher, Software Engineer, Founder and
   more) and tap **Finish**.
The chat opens. **Use a different account** on the WhatsApp step leaves sign-up and goes back to the sign-in screen.

## The main screen (the chat)
- Top left **☰** (menu) opens the side menu.
- An empty new chat shows the Classgrid logo, a greeting with your name and your plan (for example "Free plan ·
  **Upgrade**"). Tap **Upgrade** to see the plans.
- Top right on an empty chat: the ghost icon starts an incognito chat. In a chat with messages: the pencil icon
  starts a new chat and **⋮** opens the chat options (Share, Rename, Pin, Archive, Copy, Delete and more).
- The message box at the bottom (**Ask Classgrid**): **+** adds a photo from the **Camera**, **Photos**, **Files**,
  your Library, and opens Plugins, Images, Skills and Connected apps. The model button picks the AI model. The mic
  dictates. While the box is empty, the round button next to the mic starts voice mode (talk with Classgrid out
  loud); once you type, the send arrow takes its place.

## The side menu
Tap **☰** at the top left (or swipe it closed to the left). From top to bottom:
- **Classgrid** title with a search icon: search all your chats.
- **Projects**, **Library**, **Docs**, **Schedule**, **Calls**, **Remote PC**.
- Your chats: pinned chats, today and earlier ones. Tap one to open it, long-press it for Share, Rename, Pin chat,
  Archive, Move to project and Delete. **View all** lists every chat.
- At the bottom: your photo and first name (opens **Profile**), the gear (opens **Settings**) and **New Chat**.

## Good to know
- Back (the arrow at the top left or Android's back) from a side menu page returns to the chat.
- Settings holds your plan, billing, usage, devices, emails, notifications, privacy, color mode and log out.
- You can keep up to 3 accounts on one phone and switch between them (see "Accounts: add, switch and remove
  (phone app)").
