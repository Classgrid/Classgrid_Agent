# The message box (phone app)

Everything in the box at the bottom of the chat: the **+** menu (photos, files, camera, library, pictures, web search,
plugins), picking an app with **@**, pasted text, dictation, the model button and voice mode.

## What the box shows
- Empty and not tapped: one slim row with **+**, **Ask Classgrid**, the mic (**Dictate**) and a round waves button
  (**Talk with Classgrid**, opens voice mode).
- Tapped: the text on top, then **+**, the model button (the model's logo and short name), the mic and **Send**.
- While an answer is written, a square **Stop generating** button shows.
- When the AI is using helper agents, a chip like "2 agents" shows in the box; tap it for the **Agent map**.

## How to add photos or files
1. Tap **+**. The **Add to chat** sheet opens.
2. Tap **Camera** (take a photo), **Photos** (pick from the gallery) or **Files** (any file).
3. The file shows in the box at once with an upload bar. A file that fails shows a red **Failed** chip: tap it to
   try again, or tap its **X** to remove it.
4. Type your message and tap **Send**.
- How many files one message can carry depends on your plan. When it is full the tiles are greyed and the sheet says so.

## How to add files from your Classgrid Library
1. Tap **+**, then **Add from library**.
2. Use **All**, **Images**, **Documents** or **Other** to filter. Tap files to tick them.
3. Tap **Attach** (it shows how many files).

## How to make a picture or draw a sketch
- Tap **+**, then **Create image**. **@Create image** goes into the box; type what to draw and send.
- Tap **+**, then **Sketch**. Draw with the pens (black, red, blue, orange) or the eraser, use **Undo** or **Clear**,
  then tap **Attach**. The drawing is added to your message.

## How to use web search or deep research
- There is no separate web search icon. Tap **+**, then **Web search** (or **Deep research** for a detailed report).
  The words **@Web search** go into the box; type your question and send.

## How to pick an app or plugin with @
1. In the box, type **@**. A menu opens over the box.
2. Type a few letters to filter. It lists **Add photos & files**, **Add from library**, **Create image**, **Sketch**,
   **Web search**, **Deep research**, then your connected plugins.
3. Tap one. A plugin or search shows in the box as its logo and name; backspace removes it in one go.
- Your connected plugins are also under **Connected** in the **+** sheet.

## The AI Hub rows in the + sheet
- **Plugins**: opens the plugins screen to connect apps.
- **Images**: every picture the AI made for you.
- **Skills**: opens your skills.
- **Connected apps**: apps that can use your Classgrid account; you can copy the link and **Remove access**.

## Pasted text
- Pasting a very long text does not fill the box: it becomes a **Pasted text** card above it. Tap the card to read
  or change it (**Save changes** or **Cancel**); tap its **X** to remove it. There is a limit on how many long texts one
  message can hold and how long one can be; the app says so if you go past it.
- In the sent message the card shows as **Pasted text.txt**; tap it to read the text.

## Dictation (talk instead of typing)
1. Tap the mic. A red bar with a timer shows while it listens.
2. Tap the red stop button. What you said is added after what you typed.

## Good to know
- The model button opens **Select model** (see the models page). It is shown once the box is open.
- Sharing text or files to Classgrid from another app (the phone's share menu) puts them in the chat box.
