# Chat basics (phone app)

How to chat with Classgrid AI in the Android app: start a chat, send, stop an answer, edit, copy, rate, retry and
have an answer read aloud. The chat screen is the first screen the app opens on.

## How to start a new chat
1. In a chat that has messages, tap the pencil icon at the top right (**New chat**).
2. Or open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap **New Chat** at the bottom.
3. An empty chat shows the Classgrid logo and a greeting. Type in the box at the bottom.

## How to start an incognito chat
1. Start a new chat (above). On the empty chat, tap the ghost icon at the top right.
2. The screen shows **Incognito chat**. Incognito chats are not saved to your history or search and are deleted when
   you close them.
3. To leave it, tap the **X** at the top right.

## How to send a message
1. Tap **Ask Classgrid** at the bottom. The box opens and the keyboard comes up.
2. Type your message and tap the round arrow button (**Send**).
3. The phone gives one light tap (vibration) when the message is sent, and the box folds back to one slim row.
- You can type and send while an answer is still being written. The new message waits above the box and goes when
  the answer ends. Tap **Send now** under it to stop the answer and send it at once, or tap its **X** to remove it.

## How to stop an answer
1. While the answer is being written, the right button of the box is a square (**Stop generating**). Tap it.
2. What was written stays, with the card **Classgrid's response was interrupted.** under it.

## How to edit and resend your message
1. Long-press your own message (the grey bubble on the right).
2. Tap **Edit**. Change the words in the bubble.
3. Tap **Send** (or **Cancel**). The edited message is sent again and gets a new answer; the messages after it are
   replaced.
- Edit is not offered while an answer is being written, or on someone else's message in a shared chat.

## How to copy
- Your message: long-press it, then tap **Copy**.
- An answer: tap the copy icon under it (**Copy response**). The app says **Message copied to clipboard!**
- The whole chat or its link: tap ⋮ at the top right, tap **Copy**, then **Copy link**, **Copy chat** or
  **Copy chat ID**.

## How to rate an answer (like or dislike)
1. Under the answer, tap the thumbs up (**Helpful**). Tap it again to take it back.
2. Or tap the thumbs down (**Not helpful**). The **Submit feedback** sheet opens: pick **What type of issue?**
   (for example **Not factually correct**), add details and files if you want, then tap **Submit Feedback**.

## How to retry an answer
- Tap the round arrow (**Retry**) under the last answer. The last message is sent again and the answer is made again.

## How to reply to one answer
1. Under the answer, tap the reply arrow (**Reply to message**).
2. **REPLYING TO AI** shows above the box. Type and send; the answer is quoted in your message. Tap its **X** to cancel.

## How to have an answer read aloud
1. Under the answer, tap the speaker icon (**Read aloud**).
2. A player shows above the box: pause and play, back 15 seconds, forward 15 seconds, the speed (1x, 1.25x, 1.5x,
   2x, 0.75x) and a close button. At the end you can play it again or rate the answer.
- It reads in the voice you picked in voice mode or in **Settings**, **Voices**.

## Long-press menus on a message
- Your message: **Copy** and **Edit**.
- Someone else's message in a shared chat: **Copy** and **Report message**.
- Answers have no long-press menu; use the row of icons under them.

## Good to know
- The light tap on send, copy, long-press and Stop can be turned off: open the side menu, tap the gear (Settings) at
  the bottom, then switch off **Haptic feedback**.
- With no internet, a notice shows above the box.
- When your plan's limit is reached, a card says when it comes back, with **See plans** or **Top up**, and **Dismiss**.
- If a chat ends with **Chat ended**, tap **Start a new chat**.
- The chat menu (⋮ at the top right) also has **Files in chat**, **Conversation outline** (every message, tap to jump
  there) and **Your questions** (when the chat has any).
