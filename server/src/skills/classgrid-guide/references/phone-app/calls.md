# Calls (phone app)

Calls lists every WhatsApp call you had with Classgrid AI, plus the tasks your WhatsApp messages or emails to
agent@classgrid.in started, and what the AI did for each one after it. The list is read-only.

## How a call is made
1. Your WhatsApp number must be verified. If it is not: open the side menu (tap ☰), tap the gear at the bottom
   (Settings), tap **Verify WhatsApp number** and follow the steps (say Hi to Classgrid on WhatsApp at
   +91 81492 77038, enter your number, **Send code**, enter the code, **Verify**).
2. Call the Classgrid WhatsApp number from that phone in WhatsApp.
3. Talk to Classgrid AI. If you give it a task, it works on it after the call. The call shows in **Calls**.
- There is no "New call" button in the app. Calls are made from WhatsApp.

## How to see your calls
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap **Calls**. Calls are grouped by day, newest first.
3. Each row shows where it came from (**WhatsApp call**, **WhatsApp message** or **Email to agent@classgrid.in**),
   the name, a summary line, the status, the time and what the AI did.
4. Filter with the chips: **All**, **Working**, **Needs you**, **Done**, **Failed**.
5. To search, tap the round search button at the bottom left and type in **Search calls**. It also looks in what was
   said.

## How to open a call's details
1. In **Calls**, tap the call.
2. The top shows where it came from and when, the name, the summary and facts (project, steps, time worked, WhatsApp
   messages and emails sent, model).
3. Three tabs:
   - **Transcript** (for a call) or **Message** (for a message or email): what was said, line by line with the time,
     and the AI's notes (**Saved a task**, **Asked Classgrid**, tools it used). Use **Search in the transcript** and
     the up and down arrows to jump between matches.
   - **What the AI did**: each run of the work after the call and every step inside it.
   - **Details**: status, times, models, project, tools used and everything sent to you.
4. To copy or share the transcript, tap ⋮ at the top right, then **Copy transcript** or **Share transcript**.

## What the statuses mean
- **On the call**: the call is going on now. **Starting** / **Working**: the AI is working on it.
- **Continues later**: the AI paused on purpose and continues at the time shown.
- **Waiting for you**: the AI asked you something on WhatsApp; reply there.
- **Paused: limit reached**: your plan's limit is used up; it continues when it comes back.
- **Answered on the call**: no task was saved; the questions were answered on the call.
- **Done**, **Failed**, **Failed, trying again**, **Stopped** (not finished in 48 hours).

## Good to know
- The list and a call's page refresh by themselves while a call is live or its work is running.
- The link Classgrid sends on WhatsApp after a call opens that call's page in the app.
- The app keeps the text transcript and the summary. There is no audio recording to play in the app.
- Calls cannot be deleted or edited from the phone.
