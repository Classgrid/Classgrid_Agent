# Share Grid: chats with other people (phone app)

Share Grid lets a few people work in one chat with Classgrid AI together. The owner invites people with an invite
link, by email, or from their Grids (the people they message with in Classgrid Grids). Everyone in the chat sees
all messages and can ask the AI.

## How to join with an invite link
1. Tap the invite link you got (it starts with chat.classgrid.in/grid/join/), for example in WhatsApp, an email or
   the browser. It opens in the Classgrid app. If you are signed out, sign in first; the invite opens after.
2. The screen says who invited you, with the chat's name and **People** (how many are in it).
3. Tap **Join chat**. The chat opens.
4. Or tap **Decline**. You are not added to the chat.
- Already in the chat: tap **Open chat**.
- **This invite was already used**, **This chat already has** a full number of people, or **This invite can't be
  opened** mean you cannot join with that link. Ask the owner for a new one.
- There is no join code to type in. Joining is only with the link or an invite from the owner.

## How to invite people to your chat
1. Open the chat.
2. Tap ⋮ at the top right, then **Share**.
3. Scroll to **Share Grid**. Under **Invite people** it says how many more people can join.
4. Choose one way:
   - **Copy invite link**, then send it to them.
   - **Add from Grids**: search, tick people, tap **Invite** (or **Invite** with the number of people).
   - Type emails (a comma between them) in the email box and tap **Invite**.
5. To stop the link from working, tap **Turn off invite link**.

## What you see in a shared chat
- In the side menu chat list the chat has a filled star.
- A message someone else sent has their name and photo above it.
- A line shows when someone joins the chat.
- While the AI answers someone else, the box says so and your message waits until it is done (you can keep typing).
- Long-press someone else's message to report it.

## How to see who is in the chat
1. Open the chat, tap ⋮, then **Share**.
2. Under **People** you see each person, **Owner** or **Member**, and how many messages they sent.
3. **History** lists the invites sent.

## How to remove someone or leave
- Owner: open **Share**, and under **People** tap the remove button next to the person.
- Member: open **Share** and tap the leave button next to your own name. Or open the side menu (tap ☰), long-press the
  chat and tap **Leave chat**.

## Good to know
- Only the owner can invite and remove people.
- A chat has a top number of people; the sheet shows how many more can join.
- A shared chat you joined cannot be renamed, archived or moved to a project by you.

## Not on the phone
- Grids messages themselves (direct messages and groups with people) have no screen in the phone app. Use
  chat.classgrid.in.
