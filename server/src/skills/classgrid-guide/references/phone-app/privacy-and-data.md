# Privacy and your data (phone app)

How to control what other people see on your profile, chat without saving history (incognito), and delete chats or
the data kept on your phone in the Classgrid phone app.

## How to choose what others see on your profile
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap the gear (**Settings**).
2. Tap **Privacy** ("Choose what other people can see on your profile.").
3. Flip the switches:
   - **Show Email**: let others see your email address on your profile.
   - **Show Hobbies**: let others see your hobbies on your profile.
   - **Show Bio**: let others see your bio on your profile.
Each change saves at once ("Privacy setting saved.").

## How to start an incognito chat
1. Start a new chat (tap **New Chat** in the side menu, or the pencil icon at the top right of a chat).
2. On the empty new chat, tap the ghost icon at the top right.
3. "You're incognito" shows. Type and send as usual.
4. To leave, tap the **X** at the top right. A normal new chat opens.
- Incognito chats are not saved to your history or search. They are deleted when you close them.

## How to delete a chat
From the open chat:
1. Tap **⋮** at the top right of the chat.
2. Tap **Delete**, then **Delete chat** to confirm.
From the side menu or the full chat list:
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰) or tap **View all**.
2. Long-press the chat and tap **Delete**, then **Delete chat**.
- This permanently deletes the chat and all its messages. It cannot be undone.
- A chat someone else shared with you shows **Leave chat** instead of **Delete**.

## How to hide a chat without deleting it (archive)
1. Long-press the chat in the side menu (or tap **⋮** in the open chat) and tap **Archive**.
2. To find it again: **Settings** > **Archived chats**. There tap **Unarchive** to bring it back, or **Delete** to
   remove it for good.

## How to delete the data saved on this phone
If **Save for offline** is on, your chats, Library and files are also kept on the phone.
1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete saved data** (under **Save for offline**), then **Delete**.
- This only removes the copy on the phone. Everything stays in your account, and it is saved again while
  **Save for offline** is on. Turn **Save for offline** off to stop keeping a copy on the phone.

## Delete everything
To delete your whole account and all its data, see "Delete your account (phone app)".

## Good to know
- Privacy policy: **Settings** > **Privacy policy** opens it in the browser.
- Who can see a shared chat or doc is set when you share it (the **Share** option of the chat).
