# Troubleshooting (phone app)

Common problems in the Classgrid phone app and what to do about each one.

## No internet / offline
- Above the message box: "The Internet connection appears to be offline." It goes away by itself when the phone is
  back online (the X hides it until the next time).
- Check Wi-Fi or mobile data, then send again.
- To read your chats and Library without internet, turn on **Settings > Save for offline** while you are online. The
  chats, Library and their files are then kept on the phone.

## An answer stops or does not come
- "The connection timed out or was dropped. Please try again or ask a shorter question.": the connection broke. Tap
  **Retry** (the round arrow under the last answer), or ask a shorter question.
- If you leave the app while an answer is being written, the app picks up the rest when you come back to it.
- "Classgrid's response was interrupted.": the answer was stopped with the stop button. Send again or tap **Retry**.
- "This answer stopped before any words came back.": tap **Retry**.
- A chat that does not open shows **Try again**. Tap it.
- If your plan's limit is used up, a card says so in place of the answer, with when it comes back, and buttons such as
  **See plans**, **Top up**, **Buy credits** or **Switch to Auto**. See your limits in **Settings > Usage**.
- To see if Classgrid itself has a problem: **Settings > Platform Status**.

## Sign-in problems
- "Wrong email or password.": check the password, or tap **Forgot password?** (a reset link is emailed and works for
  10 minutes), or tap **Email me a code instead**.
- "Enter the 6-digit code.": type all 6 digits from the email. Wait for the resend timer to ask for a new code.
- Signing in on a new phone emails you a one-time code; enter it once and the phone is trusted after that.
- "This email is not verified yet.": use **Email me a code instead**.
- Signed in on too many devices for your plan: the sign-in screen lists your devices. Tap **Log out** on one you do
  not use, then sign in again.
- You can also sign in with **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**.

## A plugin will not connect
1. Open the plugin's page again (Settings > **Plugins** > the app) and tap **Connect**.
2. Finish the whole sign-in in the browser that opens and allow access, then return to Classgrid. If you come back
   before finishing, the app stops waiting after a few seconds.
3. "Could not open the browser. Please try again.": the sign-in page needs a web browser on the phone. Make sure one
   is installed and turned on, then tap **Connect** again.
4. Sanity CMS: "Failed to verify Sanity credentials. Check your API keys." means the Project ID or API Token is wrong.
5. An added connector marked **Needs login**: tap **Log in**. Marked **Can't connect**: tap **Try again**. Marked
   **Turned off**: tap **⋯** > **Turn on**.
6. The Plugins list shows "Something went wrong.": tap **Try again**, or pull the list down.
7. Still failing: **Disconnect** the plugin (**⋯** > **Disconnect**) and connect it again.

## Remote PC does not answer
- A red dot means the computer is not connected: open Classgrid on the computer, make sure it is on and awake.
- "Could not send it to your computer. Try again.": check the computer, then send again.
- Messages refused after **Disconnect this computer**: tap **⋮** > **Connect this computer**.

## App out of date
- Updates come only through Google Play. Open
  https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.classgrid.mobile and tap **Update** if it shows.
- Your installed version is in **Settings > Version**. What changed in each release: **Settings > Changelog**.

## Clearing things
- Free up phone space: **Settings > Delete saved data** (shown under **Save for offline** when something is saved).
  It only removes the copies on the phone; everything stays in your account.
- Start fresh on this phone: **Settings > Log out**, then sign in again.

## Getting help
- **Settings > Support** opens the support page to send a ticket.
