# Usage (phone app)

The Usage page shows how much of each of your plan's limits you have used and when each one refills. Use it when
Classgrid stops answering, or to see how much is left.

## How to open Usage
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap the gear icon (**Settings**) at the bottom of the side menu.
3. Tap **Usage**.

You can also open it from Settings, **Plan**, then **Usage** under **Past the plan**.

## What the Usage page shows
- At the top: **Your weekly limit resets <day, date, time>.**
- **Session limit**: all models, counted from your first message for a few hours. The line under the bar says when it
  resets (for example **Resets in 2 h 10 min**).
- **Weekly limit**: all models, for the week. Tap it to open its own limits:
  - **Claude limit**: Sonnet, Opus and Haiku, weekly.
  - **Fable limit**: a share of your Claude limit, weekly.
- **WhatsApp and voice** (tap to open): **WhatsApp messages limit**, **WhatsApp calls limit**, **Voice limit**
  (minutes talking with the AI) and **Read-aloud limit** (answers read out loud).
- **Projects** (tap to open): **Cloud computer limit**, the time your code, projects and live browser ran in the cloud.
- **Images** (tap to open): **Images and photo edits**.
- **Storage** (tap to open): **Library**, **Files**, **Images** and **Cloud projects**.

Each limit shows **<number>% used** on the right and a bar. A closed section shows the highest percent inside it. A
limit that is used up turns red, and its section opens by itself. **No limit** means nothing limits it. Limits your
plan does not include are not shown.

## When limits refill
- Session limit: a few hours after your first message. The row shows how long is left.
- Weekly limit, Claude limit and Fable limit: once a week, at the time shown at the top of the page.
- Voice limit and Read-aloud limit: every day at midnight.
- WhatsApp, cloud computer and images: on the date shown under each bar.

## What to do when a limit is reached
When you reach a limit in a chat, a card shows **You've reached your <plan> plan's limit** and **It comes back on
<date, time>.** Its buttons:
- **See plans** (Free plan): opens the upgrade page to buy a higher plan.
- **Top up** (paid plan): opens Billing to buy credits. Credits keep chats, Claude, Fable, images, messages and voice
  going past the plan's limits.
- **Dismiss**: hides the card. Wait until the time shown and send again.

Other places with an **Upgrade** button when a limit stops you:
- Voice mode shows **Upgrade** when voice is not in your plan or its limit is used up.
- A model with a lock in the model picker shows **Upgrade to use <plan>**, with **Upgrade** and **Cancel**.
- A file that is too big for your plan shows the reason under the file in the message box, with **Upgrade**.

## Good to know
- The page shows percents only, not credit numbers.
- Pull down on the page to refresh it. It also refreshes when you come back to it.
- Credits you bought or were given are used after your included usage. Your balance is on the Billing page.
- If the page says **Could not load your usage**, check the connection and tap **Try again**.
