# Connected apps (phone app)

Connected apps lists outside apps, like Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor, that you allowed to use your Classgrid account.
It is the other direction from Plugins: Plugins let Classgrid AI use your apps; Connected apps are apps that use
Classgrid. Use it to see what each app can do and to remove its access.

## How to open Connected apps
From Settings:
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap the gear at the bottom, next to your name.
3. Tap **Connected apps**.

From the message box:
1. In any chat, tap **+** at the bottom left of the message box.
2. Under **AI Hub**, tap **Connected apps**. It opens inside the same sheet; the back arrow returns to **Add to chat**.

## What is listed
- A line like "2 apps have access to your account".
- One box per app: its logo and name, **Verified** or **Unverified app**, "Has access to:" followed by what it may do,
  "Access given on <date> · Last active <when>", and **Can change data** when it has a permission that changes data.
- With no apps: "No apps have access to your account".
- Pull the list down to refresh it.

## How to see what an app can do
1. Open Connected apps.
2. Tap the app's box. It opens to **What <app> can do**: every permission, with "changes data" next to the ones that
   change things. It also shows where the app sends you back after sign-in, its website, and its **App id**.

## How to remove an app's access
1. Open Connected apps and tap the app's box.
2. Tap **Remove access**.
3. In the **Remove access** dialog, tap **Remove access** again.
4. The app leaves the list and a message says "<app> can no longer access your account".

## How to connect Classgrid in another app
1. Open Connected apps.
2. Under **Connect Classgrid in another app**, tap **Copy** to copy the link.
3. In Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any app that supports MCP, add Classgrid as a connector with that link. That app then
   asks you to sign in to Classgrid and shows what it wants to access; you choose what to allow.

## Good to know
- Removing access signs the app out at once. You can connect it again later from that app.
- **Unverified app** means the app named itself and Classgrid has not checked who made it. Remove it if you do not
  recognise it.
- If the list does not load ("Couldn't load your connected apps."), check your internet and tap **Try again**.
