# Storage (phone app)

The Storage page shows how much of your Library's space is used, out of what your plan includes, and what is using
it. Use it when uploads stop with "Storage is full" or to free space.

## How to open Storage
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap the gear icon (**Settings**) at the bottom of the side menu.
3. Tap **Storage**.

## What the Storage page shows
- **Library storage**: your plan name and how much space it includes (or **Unlimited**), how much you have used, a
  bar with **<number>% used** and how much is **left**.
- When it is almost full: **Your storage is almost full. Delete files in the Library, or upgrade your plan for more
  space.**
- **What's using your storage**:
  - **Uploaded files**: files you added to the Library or to a chat, with their count and size.
  - **AI-generated files**: pictures, PDFs and documents Classgrid AI made, with their count and size.
  - **Trash**: AI-generated files you deleted, kept for 30 days.
- **Manage files in Library** at the bottom opens the Library.

The numbers change by themselves when you upload, delete, restore or empty the Trash. Pull down to refresh.

## How to free space
1. On the Storage page, tap **Uploaded files** or **AI-generated files**. The Library opens on those files.
2. Find a file. Tap the three dots next to it (or press and hold the file).
3. Tap **Delete**, then **Delete** again in **Delete file?**.
   - An uploaded file is deleted for good and its space is freed at once.
   - An AI-generated file goes to the Trash (the app says **Moved to Trash**). It still uses space until the Trash is
     emptied or 30 days pass.
4. To free the space of AI-generated files at once, empty the Trash (next section).

## How to empty the Trash
1. On the Storage page, tap **Trash** (or in the Library, tap the trash icon at the top right).
2. Tap the red trash icon at the top right (**Empty the Trash**).
3. Tap **Delete for good**. The files cannot be brought back.

## How to bring a file back from the Trash
1. Open **Trash** as above.
2. Tap **Restore** next to the file. The app says **Back in your Library**.

## Good to know
- Space is shared by everything in the Library: uploaded files and files Classgrid AI made.
- When storage is full, uploading says **Storage is full. Delete some files first.**
- When a file goes over your plan's limits, the message has an **Upgrade** button that opens the upgrade page.
  Higher plans include more space (see your plan in Settings, **Plan**).
- To keep a copy before deleting, open the file's menu and tap **Download** (it saves to Downloads/Classgrid).
- The Usage page (Settings, **Usage**, then tap **Storage**) shows the same Library numbers and your
  **Cloud projects** count.
- You can also reach the Library from the side menu: tap ☰, then **Library**.
