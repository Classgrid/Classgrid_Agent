# Using your apps in a chat (phone app)

Once an app is connected in Plugins, you can ask Classgrid AI to use it in any chat, in plain words, or pick the app
with @ so the AI knows exactly which one you mean.

## How to ask the AI to use an app
1. Connect the app first (see "Plugins (phone app)").
2. In a chat, type what you want, for example "Read my unread emails from today" or "What's on my calendar for
   tomorrow?".
3. Tap the send arrow. The AI picks the right app and the right action itself.

## How to pick an app with @
With the keyboard:
1. In the message box, type **@**. A menu opens above the box.
2. Keep typing to filter, or scroll. Your connected apps are listed after the menu's own items (**Add photos &
   files**, **Add from library**, **Create image**, **Sketch**, **Web search**, **Deep research**).
3. Tap the app. "@<app>" goes into the box, shown with the app's logo.
4. Type the rest of your request and send.

With the + button:
1. Tap **+** at the bottom left of the message box. The **Add to chat** sheet opens.
2. Under **Connected**, tap the app. "@<app>" goes into the box.
3. Type your request and send.

Picking with @ helps when two apps do the same thing, for example Gmail and Microsoft Outlook.

## What you see while it works
- One line under your question shows what the AI is doing right now, in plain words with the app's name and logo,
  for example "Checking your calendar" with "Google Calendar" in grey after it, or "Sending an email" with "Gmail".
- Tap that line to open all the steps in a sheet. Tap a step to see **Request and response**: what was asked of the
  app and what came back.
- When it is done, the line says how long it worked (for example "Worked for 12 sec") and the answer follows.
- A step that failed says so (for example "Couldn't send email"), and the AI usually tells you why in its answer.
- To stop it, tap the stop button where the send arrow was.

## How to approve or answer when the AI asks
The AI may stop and ask you before it does something, or ask which option you want.
- A question card can open above the message box. Tap one of the numbered choices, or type your own answer in
  **Something else**, or tap **Skip**. The X closes the card without answering; you can then type your reply in the
  message box.
- A plan card shows **Approve** (with an automatic approve countdown you can cancel) and **View Plan**.
- A command card shows **Run** and **Skip**.
- If you closed a card, tap the line under the answer (for example "Approve or skip this plan") to open it again.
- If the AI simply asks in its answer (for example "Shall I send this?"), reply in the message box ("Yes, send it").

## Good to know
- The AI acts as you: an email from Gmail is sent from your address, a Slack message is posted as you.
- Ask for a draft first if you want to check something before it is sent or posted.
- If the AI says an app is not connected, open Plugins and connect it, then ask again.
