# Emails (phone app)

Two different things use email in Classgrid on the phone: the **Emails** screen in Settings (the email addresses on
your account) and emailing the agent at agent@classgrid.in (a task for Classgrid AI, shown in Calls).

## How to open the Emails screen
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap the gear at the bottom (Settings).
3. Tap **Emails**.

## What the Emails screen shows
- Your main email, marked **Main** (and **School** if it is a school email).
- Any extra emails you added, each with a **Remove** button.
- How many emails you have out of the most allowed, and the **Add email** button.
- You can sign in with any of these emails and the same password. It is one account: your usage and limits are shared.

## How to add an email
1. On the Emails screen, tap **Add email**.
2. In **Add an email**, type the email and tap **Send code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to that email and tap **Add email**.
4. If the code did not come, tap **Use another email or send a new code**.
- When the account already has the most emails allowed, **Add email** is greyed out: remove one first.
- Only one school email can be on an account.

## How to remove an email
1. On the Emails screen, tap **Remove** next to the email.
2. A note says it is removed from your account. The main email cannot be removed here.

## How to email the agent
1. From your email app, write to agent@classgrid.in with what you want done.
2. Classgrid AI works on it as a task.
3. To follow it on the phone: open the side menu (tap ☰), tap **Calls**. The task shows with **Email to
   agent@classgrid.in**. Tap it to see the **Message**, **What the AI did** and **Details** tabs.

## Good to know
- The Emails screen only manages sign-in emails. It does not list emails sent to the agent; those are in **Calls**.
- In a task from an email, the **Details** tab lists everything Classgrid sent you for it, and the facts at the top
  count emails read and sent.
