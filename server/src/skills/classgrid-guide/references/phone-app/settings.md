# Settings map (phone app)

Settings is where you manage your account, plan, devices, notifications, privacy and how the app looks. This page
lists every row from top to bottom and what it does. Other phone pages explain each one in detail.

## How to open Settings
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap the gear at the bottom, next to your name.
Settings has its own ☰ at the top left to open the side menu again.

## Account card (top)
- Your email, name and plan. Tap it to open **Switch account**: switch, add (**Add account**) or sign out accounts.
  See "Accounts: add, switch and remove (phone app)".

## Account rows
- **Profile**: your photo, name, details, WhatsApp number and password. See "Profile (phone app)".
- **Plan**: your plan and what it gives, with **Upgrade** and **Manage subscription**. See
  "Plans and upgrade (phone app)".
- **Billing**: your plan, credit balance, buying credits, low credit reminder and your past payments. See
  "Billing (phone app)".
- **Usage**: how much of your plan's limits you have used. See "Usage (phone app)".
- **Storage**: how much Library room you use out of what your plan gives. See "Storage (phone app)".
- **Archived chats**: every archived chat, grouped by project, with search, sort and filter. Unarchive or delete
  them here.
- **Devices**: where your account is signed in; log out one or all. See "Signed-in devices (phone app)".
- **Remote PC**: Remote PC notifications, your computers, and turning off Remote PC chats.
- **Emails**: your account's emails (the main one and extra ones), each confirmed with a code. **Add email** and
  **Remove**.

## AI rows
- **Skills** (shows how many you have): your skills.
- **Plugins** (shows how many are connected): connect apps and services to the AI. See "Plugins (phone app)".
- **Images**: the pictures made in your chats.
- **Connected apps**: outside apps you allowed through Classgrid.

## Look and feel
- **Color mode**: tap **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (follows the phone).
- **Font** (only on app versions that support it): **Default**, **Web**, **System** or **Serif**.

## More rows
- **Notifications**: push alerts and email notifications. See "Notifications (phone app)".
- **Privacy**: what other people see on your profile. See "Privacy and your data (phone app)".
- **Verify WhatsApp number**: only shown when your account has no verified WhatsApp number yet (you skipped it at
  sign-up). Tap **Open WhatsApp**, send the hello message, type your number with the country code, tap **Send code**,
  then type the code and tap **Verify**. The row goes away once it is verified.
- **Voices**: microphone, permissions and the voice Classgrid speaks with (the chosen voice shows on the row).
- **Haptic feedback** (switch): a light vibration when you send a message, copy something, open a long-press menu,
  flip a switch or press Stop. On by default.
- **Save for offline** (switch): keeps your chats, Library and their files on this phone so you can read them without
  internet. The row shows how much space they take. **Delete saved data** removes them from the phone only.

## Links (open in the browser)
- **Home Page**, **Changelog**, **Support** (send a support ticket), **Platform Status** (shows if Classgrid is up,
  with a colored dot).
- **Privacy policy**, **Terms of service**.
- **Version**: the app version and build number (for support).

## At the bottom
- **Log out**: signs this account out of this phone. See "Sign in and sign out (phone app)".
- **Delete Account**: deletes your account for good. See "Delete your account (phone app)".
