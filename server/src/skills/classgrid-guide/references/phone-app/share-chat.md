# Sharing a chat (phone app)

Two ways to share a chat in the Android app: a read-only link anyone can open, and **Share Grid**, which brings up to
a few people into the same chat to talk with the AI together. Also how to open a chat someone shared with you.

## How to open the Share sheet
1. Open the chat. Tap ⋮ at the top right.
2. Tap **Share**.
- Or open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left), long-press the chat and tap **Share**.
- The sheet shows the chat's title and a preview of its first messages.

## How to share a read-only link
1. In the Share sheet, tap one of the round buttons:
   - **Copy link**: makes the public link and copies it (**Public link copied to clipboard!**).
   - **WhatsApp** or **LinkedIn**: makes the link and opens that app with it.
   - **Email**: emails the chat (a tick shows when it is sent).
2. Anyone with the link can read the chat. They cannot write in it.

## How to bring people into the chat (Share Grid)
Only the chat's owner sees **Invite people**. It says how many more people can join.
1. In the Share sheet, scroll to **Share Grid**.
2. Pick one way:
   - Tap **Copy invite link** and send it to them.
   - Tap **Add from Grids**, search and tick people from your Grids, then tap **Invite** (or **Invite N people**).
   - Type one or more emails (split by commas) in the email field and tap **Invite**.
3. **People** lists everyone in the chat (**Owner** or **Member**, with how many messages each sent).
   **History** lists every invite with **Pending**, **Accepted**, **Declined** or **Turned off**.

## Who can see a shared chat
- Read-only link: anyone who has the link.
- Share Grid: only the people in **People**. Each person's message shows their name and photo. While the AI answers
  someone else, a line says so and your message waits until it is done.

## How to stop sharing
- Invite link: in the Share sheet, tap **Turn off invite link**. The link stops working for new people.
- Remove a person (owner only): tap the person icon at the right of their row in **People**.
- Leave a chat someone shared with you: tap the person icon on your own row (**Leave chat**), or long-press the chat in
  the side menu and tap **Leave chat**.

## How to open a chat someone shared with you
- A read-only link (share.classgrid.in): tap it on the phone. It opens in the app with the title and **Shared by**.
  At the top: **Share**, **Copy link** and **Open in browser**.
- A Share Grid invite link (a chat.classgrid.in link with grid/join in it): tap it. The app shows who invited you; tap **Join chat**
  or **Decline**. If you are already in it, tap **Open chat**.
- Chats you joined show in your chat list with a star.

## How to report someone's message in a shared chat
1. Long-press their message. Tap **Report message**.
2. Pick a reason and tap **Send report**.

## Good to know
- If a link opens in the browser instead of the app, Android may need Classgrid set to open these links (in the
  phone's app settings, "Open by default").
- If you are removed from a shared chat, the app says **You're no longer in this shared chat.** and opens a new chat.
- A read-only link is a copy of the chat as it was when the link was made. Messages sent later are not in it; make a
  new link to share them.
- There is no button to turn off a read-only link once it is made.
