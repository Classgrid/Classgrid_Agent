# Your chats: list, search, rename, pin, archive, delete (phone app)

Where your past chats are in the Android app and how to find, open, rename, pin, archive, bring back, delete or put a
chat on the phone's Home screen.

## Where the chats are
- Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left). Under **Projects**, **Library**, **Docs**, **Schedule**, **Calls** and
  **Remote PC** is your chat list: **Pinned**, **Today** and **Previous** (tap **Show N more** for older ones).
- Tap **View all** at the end of the list to open **Recents**, the full list on its own screen.
- A blue dot means a new answer you have not read. A star means a chat shared with other people. Moving dots mean
  the chat is still answering.

## How to open a chat
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap the chat. It opens in the chat screen.

## How to search your chats
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap the search icon next to **Classgrid** at the top. **Recents** opens with the search field at the bottom.
3. Type words from the chat's title. Tap a result to open it.
- Search looks at chat titles only.

## How to rename a chat
1. In the side menu or **Recents**, long-press the chat. Tap **Rename**.
2. Type the new name and tap **Save**.
- Or, in the open chat, tap ⋮ at the top right, then **Rename**.

## How to pin or unpin a chat
1. Long-press the chat in the side menu or **Recents**. Tap **Pin chat** (or **Unpin chat**).
2. In **Recents** you can also swipe the chat to the left and tap **Pin**.
- Or, in the open chat, tap ⋮, then **Pin** or **Unpin**. Pinned chats show under **Pinned**. Only a few chats can be
  pinned; the app says when you reach the most.

## How to archive a chat
1. Long-press the chat. Tap **Archive**. Or in **Recents** swipe it to the left and tap **Archive**.
2. Or, in the open chat, tap ⋮, then **Archive**. A new chat opens.
- Archived chats leave the list but are not deleted.

## How to see and unarchive archived chats
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap the gear at the bottom (Settings).
2. Tap **Archived chats**. Chats are grouped by project, with a count.
3. Use **Search archived chats**, the sort pill (**Updated**, **Created**, **Alphabetical**) and the project pill
   (**All projects**, **No project** or one project).
4. Tap a chat to open it, tap **Unarchive** to put it back in your list, or **Delete** to delete it for good.
- Quick way: in the side menu, tap the filter icon on the first section line (**Display options**) and turn on
  **Archived Only**. Long-press a chat there for **Unarchive**.

## How to delete a chat
1. Long-press the chat. Tap **Delete**, then **Delete chat** to confirm.
2. Or, in the open chat, tap ⋮, then **Delete**, then **Delete chat**.
- Deleting cannot be undone. For a chat someone shared with you the item is **Leave chat**: it leaves your list but
  stays for the others.

## How to add a chat to the Home screen
1. Open the chat. Tap ⋮ at the top right.
2. Tap **Add to Home screen**. Android asks to confirm; then the chat has its own icon on the Home screen.

## How to move a chat into a project
1. Long-press the chat. Tap **Move to project**.
2. Pick a project, or tap **New project**. Projects need GitHub connected; if it is not, tap **Open Connectors**.

## Display options
- Tap the filter icon (**Display options**) at the top of **Recents**, or on the first section line in the side menu.
- **Sort Conversations**: **Last Updated**, **Created** or **Name**. **Unread only**, **Show time**, **Archived Only**,
  and **Mark All as Read**.

## Good to know
- Chats inside a project are not in this list or its search. Open the side menu, tap **Projects**, open the project
  and use its **Chats** tab.
- In **Recents**, pull down to refresh.
- Incognito chats are never in the list.
