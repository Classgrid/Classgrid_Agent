# Skills and custom instructions (phone app)

Skills are rules Classgrid AI (the Agent) follows in every new chat, like "Always answer in English". The Skills
screen also holds built-in skills you switch on or off, how the Agent answers, and what it should know about you.
Everything here is the same on the website and the desktop app.

## How to open Skills
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap the gear at the bottom (Settings).
3. Tap **Skills** (it shows how many skills you have).
- Tapping a saved-skill card in a chat, or the **Memory updated** note under an answer, also opens Skills.

## How to create a skill
1. Open Skills.
2. Tap **+** at the top right, or **Add a skill** (**Add your first skill** when you have none).
3. Type a **Name** and the **Instructions** (what the Agent should always do).
4. Tap **Save skill**. A note says **Skill added**.
- You can also ask Classgrid AI in a chat to remember something as a skill; it shows here after.

## How to edit a skill
1. Open Skills. Under **Your skills**, tap the skill.
2. Change the **Name** or **Instructions** in **Edit skill**.
3. Tap **Save**.
- Skills marked **Default** come with Classgrid: tap to read them; they cannot be changed or deleted.

## How to delete a skill
1. Open Skills and tap the skill under **Your skills**.
2. Tap the red trash icon at the top of **Edit skill**.
3. Confirm in **Delete this skill?**. It is deleted for good and the Agent stops using it in new chats.

## How to turn built-in skills on or off
1. Open Skills and scroll to **Built-in skills**.
2. Flip the switch next to a skill, for example **Email and WhatsApp together**, **Meet links for meetings**,
   **Save reports to Drive**, **Slack alerts**, **Calendar reminders**, **Notion pages**, **YouTube tutorials**,
   **Gmail first** or **Regional translation**. It saves at once.
- Your own skills have no switch: to stop one, delete it.

## How to set how the Agent answers
1. Open Skills and scroll to **How the Agent answers**.
2. Tap a row and pick an option:
   - **Tone**: Professional, Friendly, Balanced, Candid, Quirky or Efficient.
   - **Length**: Detailed, Balanced or Concise.
   - **Format**: Markdown and lists, Plain text, Code-heavy or Visual (tables).
   - **Emoji**: More emoji, Default or No emoji.
   - **Explanation level**: Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced.

## How to add custom instructions about you
1. Open Skills and scroll to **About you**.
2. Tap **Nickname**, **Your role**, **About you** or **How to reply**.
3. Type your text and tap **Save**.
- **How to reply** is for your own instructions: formatting rules, topics to avoid or anything else.

## Good to know
- Skills and these choices apply to every new chat, on the phone, the website and the desktop app.
- Pull the Skills screen down to refresh it.
