# Sign in and sign out (phone app)

Sign-in and sign-up are the same screen in the Classgrid phone app. You can use Google, GitHub, or your email (with
a password or an emailed code). This page covers every way in, the codes, the errors you can see, and logging out.

## How to sign in with Google
1. On the sign-in screen tap **Continue with Google**.
2. Pick your Google account in the phone's Google window.
3. The app opens. If there is no Classgrid account for that Google email yet, **Create your account** opens with
   your name filled in: tap **Continue** and follow the sign-up steps below.

## How to sign in with GitHub
1. Tap **Continue with GitHub**. Your browser opens the GitHub sign-in.
2. Sign in on GitHub and approve. The browser sends you back to the app ("Signing you in").
3. While you are in the browser the app shows "Finish in your browser, then come back here." with **Cancel** to stop.

## How to sign in with email and password
1. Type your email in **Email address** and tap **Continue**.
2. **Enter your password** opens. Type it (the eye icon shows or hides it) and tap **Continue**.
3. On a phone your account has not used before, **Confirm it's you** opens: type the 6-digit code emailed to you.
   You are not asked again on this phone.

## How to sign in with an emailed code (no password)
1. Type your email and tap **Continue**.
2. On the password step tap **Email me a code instead**. (An account that never set a password, for example one made
   with Google, gets the code straight away.)
3. **Check your email**: type the 6-digit code. It goes through on its own once all 6 digits are in, or tap
   **Continue**. **Use your password instead** goes back to the password.

## How to reset a forgotten password
1. Type your email, tap **Continue**, then tap **Forgot password?** on the password step.
2. A reset link is emailed to you. Open it in your email and set a new password, then sign in with it.

## How to create a new account with email
1. Type your email and tap **Continue**. **Create your account** opens.
2. Type **First name** and **Last name**, tap **Continue**. A 6-digit code is emailed to you.
3. Type the code on **Check your email**.
4. **Create a password**: type it in **Password** and **Confirm password**. It needs 8 to 64 characters, an
   uppercase and a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol (@ # $ % ^ & * ! ? _ . -). Tap **Continue**.
5. Finish the WhatsApp, age and role steps (see "Getting started (phone app)").

## Codes
- Every code is 6 digits and is valid for 10 minutes. A new code can be asked for once a minute: the line
  "Didn't get it? Resend in 0:42" counts down, then **Resend code** sends a new one.
- **Use a different email** goes back to the first screen.

## Errors you can see
- "Enter a valid email address.": the email is not complete.
- "Wrong email or password.": check the password or use **Forgot password?** or **Email me a code instead**.
- "This email is not verified yet.": use **Email me a code instead**.
- "That code has expired. A code is valid for 10 minutes. Send a new one.": tap **Resend code**.
- "Your email check ran out. Enter your email again to get a new code.": start again from the email.
- "Google Play services is not available on this phone. Use your email instead."
- "Sign-in was interrupted. Please try again." or "Could not open the browser.": try GitHub again.
- A device limit message: your plan allows only so many signed-in devices. Your devices are listed under the message;
  tap **Log out** on one you no longer use, confirm, then sign in again.

## How to log out
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap the gear (**Settings**).
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Log out**.
3. Tap **Log out** in "Log out?" to confirm.

## Good to know
- After logging out, this phone stops getting that account's notifications.
- If other accounts are signed in on this phone, the app switches to the next one instead of showing sign-in.
- To sign out other phones or computers, use Settings > **Devices** (see "Signed-in devices (phone app)").
- By continuing you agree to Classgrid's **Terms** and **Privacy Policy** (links at the bottom of the sign-in screen).
