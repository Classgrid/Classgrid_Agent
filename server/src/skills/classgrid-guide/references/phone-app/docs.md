# Docs (phone app)

Docs are live documents (plans, notes, reports) that Classgrid AI writes for you and that you can read, edit,
comment on and share. The Docs list also holds the pages and videos the AI published (see pages-and-videos.md).

## How to open the Docs list
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap **Docs**.
3. Pick a tab: **Your docs** or **Shared with you** (each with a count).
- Pinned items are at the top, then the most recently edited.
- Each row says if it is a **Page** or a **Video** (a doc has no word), who can open it (**Private**, **Shared** or
  **Anyone with the link**) and when it was edited. On **Shared with you** it shows the owner and your role.
- Pull the list down to refresh it.

## How to search and filter the list
1. In Docs, type in the **Find a doc** box. It matches titles and owner names.
2. Tap the filter pill next to the search box (it shows the current choice, for example **All**).
3. Pick **All**, **Docs**, **Pages** or **Videos**. The phone remembers your choice.

## How to open a doc
1. In Docs, tap the doc. It opens on its own screen.
2. At the top: the comments icon, the share icon and ⋮ (**More**).
3. Pull down to check for changes. Changes by the AI or other people also show by themselves.
- A doc you have no access to shows **This doc is private** with **Request access**.

## How to create a doc
1. Start or open a chat.
2. Ask Classgrid AI to write it as a doc, for example "Write my study plan as a doc".
3. Tap the link in the answer, or find the doc later in **Docs** under **Your docs**.
- To copy an existing doc, open it, tap ⋮ and tap **Duplicate**. The copy opens.

## How to edit a doc
1. Open the doc. (The **Edit** button shows only if your role lets you edit.)
2. Tap **Edit** at the bottom right. The doc opens as Markdown text.
3. Make your changes and tap **Save** at the top right.
4. To stop without saving, tap X at the top left, then **Leave**.
- If the doc changed while you were editing, **Save your version?** asks; tap **Save anyway** to keep yours. The
  other version stays in the history.
- To rename: tap the doc's title at the top, or ⋮ then **Rename**.
- Cannot edit? Tap ⋮ then **Ask to edit**. The owner gets the request.

## How to comment
1. Open the doc. Long-press the paragraph you want to comment on.
2. Tap **Comment**. Type in **Add a comment** and tap **Comment**.
3. To read all comments, tap the comments icon at the top (or the **Comments** chip in the doc).
4. On a comment: type in **Reply** and tap **Reply**; tap the check to **Resolve**; tap the three dots for **Reopen**,
   **Report comment** or **Delete thread**. Tap **Resolved** to see resolved comments.

## How to share a doc
1. Open the doc and tap the share icon at the top.
2. Type emails in **Add people by email** (a comma between them), tap **Role** to pick **Editor**, **Commenter** or
   **Viewer**, then tap **Share**. Each person gets an email with the link.
3. Under **People with access**, tap a person to change their role, **Make owner** or **Remove**.
4. Under **General access**, choose **Only people added** or **Anyone with the link**; then set **People with the
   link** to a role.
5. Tap **Copy link** or **Share link** to send the link.
- When people ask for access, their requests show at the top of the sheet: tap **Approve** or **Decline**.

## How to delete a doc
1. Open the doc and tap ⋮.
2. Tap **Delete**, then confirm. It is deleted with its comments and older versions for everyone. This cannot be
   undone. Only the owner sees **Delete**.
- A doc someone shared with you: open it, tap the share icon, tap your own name, then **Leave this doc**.

## Other options in the ⋮ menu
- **History**: older versions. Tap one, then **Restore this version** or **Back to current**.
- **Export**: **Word**, **PDF**, **Markdown** or **Web page**, saved to Downloads.
- **Pin** / **Unpin**: keeps it at the top of your Docs list.
- **Copy link**, **Open in browser**, **Report doc** (docs owned by someone else).

## Good to know
- What you can do depends on your role: Viewer reads, Commenter also comments, Editor also edits.
- Tip from the comments sheet: you can ask Classgrid AI in the chat to answer comments or change the doc.
