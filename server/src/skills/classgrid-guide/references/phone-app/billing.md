# Billing (phone app)

The Billing page shows your current plan, your credit balance, lets you buy extra credits (top-ups) through Google
Play, set a low credit reminder, apply for student prices and see every payment on the **Invoices** tab.

## How to open Billing
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left).
2. Tap the gear icon (**Settings**) at the bottom of the side menu.
3. Tap **Billing**.

Billing also opens from Settings, **Plan**, then **Credits**, and from **Top up** on a limit card in a chat.

## What the Overview tab shows
- **Current plan**: the plan name, how it is paid and until when, and **Upgrade** (not on the top plan). A Google Play
  plan says **Renews through Google Play** or **Cancelled on Google Play, ends on its date**. A website plan says
  **Does not renew by itself**. A plan given by Classgrid says **Given to you**.
- **Additional usage**: **Credit balance** (used after your included usage) and **Low credit reminder** (On or Off).
- **Buy credits**: the credit packs (paid plans only).

## How to buy credits (top up)
Top-ups are for the Pro and Max plans. On the Free plan the page shows **Upgrade** instead: buy a plan first.
1. Open Billing (it opens on **Overview**).
2. Under **Buy credits**, tap the pack you want. Each pack shows its Google Play price and how many credits it gives.
3. Tap **Buy <price>**.
4. Google Play's payment sheet opens. Pay there.
5. The app says **<number> credits added.** and the **Credit balance** goes up.

## How to set the low credit reminder
1. Open Billing.
2. Tap **Low credit reminder**.
3. Turn on **Remind me when my credits run low**.
4. Enter the balance in **Remind me when balance is below (₹)**.
5. Under **Pack to suggest**, tap the pack you want to be reminded to buy.
6. Tap **Save**. The app says **Reminder saved.**
To stop it, turn the switch off and tap **Save** (**Reminder turned off.**).
- Nothing is charged by itself. It only reminds you, at most once a day.

## How to apply for student prices
1. Open Billing.
2. On the **Current plan** card, tap **Apply for student prices**.
3. Pick your school from the list as you type (or type its full name and tap **Continue** to describe it).
4. If your school gives students an email, add and confirm it (a code is sent to it), then tap **Continue**.
5. Tap **Choose a file** and pick a photo or scan that shows you study there (student ID card, fee receipt,
   bonafide certificate or admission letter).
6. Tap **Send application**.
- The card then shows **Under review**. Classgrid replies by email. Once approved it shows **Student** with the date.
- If it was not approved or ran out, tap **Apply again**.
- Student plans are bought on https://classgrid.in/pricing, not in the phone app.

## How to see invoices and payment history
1. Open Billing.
2. Tap the **Invoices** tab at the top.
3. Each row shows what it was (for example **Pro plan, 1 month**, **Top-up**, **Credits granted by Classgrid**,
   **Refund**), the amount, the date, credits added and the status (**Paid**, **Active**, **Used up**).
- With no payments yet it says **No invoices yet**.
- Receipts are sent to your email. Prices include GST. The phone app has no button to download an invoice.

## How to restore purchases
There is no "Restore purchases" button. The app checks your Google Play purchases by itself when it opens, when you
come back to it, and when you open Billing, Plan or the upgrade page. If something you paid for is missing, pull down
on the Billing page to check again.

## Good to know
- **Payment pending. Your credits are added once Google Play confirms it.**: Google is still waiting for the payment;
  the credits are added by themselves when it goes through.
- **Payment received. It will show here shortly.**: Google took the payment and Classgrid is confirming it.
- **Google Play is not reachable. Pull down to try again.**: check the connection, then pull down.
- Credits can be bought in the phone app even if your plan was bought on the website.
- To cancel a plan or change the card, see the Plans and upgrade page (Settings, **Plan**, **Manage subscription**).

## Not on the phone
- Buying a plan at the student price: https://classgrid.in/pricing.
