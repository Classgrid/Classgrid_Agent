# Choosing a model (phone app)

How to pick which AI model answers in the Android app, and how hard it thinks.

## How to choose a model
1. In the chat, tap **Ask Classgrid** at the bottom so the box opens.
2. Tap the model button next to **+** (it shows the model's logo and short name, for example "Auto").
3. The **Select model** sheet opens. Tap a model. The sheet closes and the button shows the new model.

## What the picker shows
- **Auto** at the top: "Picks the best model for each message". This is the default.
- Then the models in groups, with **More models** last. The models in the list change from time to time, so do not
  name them from this page: tell the user to open the list to see them.
- Each row has a short description and a tick on the chosen one. A recently added model shows **New**.
- A model your plan does not include is greyed, with a padlock and a tag naming the plans that have it. Tapping it
  opens **Upgrade to use** with **Upgrade** (opens the plans) or **Cancel**; it is not picked.

## How to change how hard it thinks
1. Open **Select model** (steps above).
2. Tap **Thinking** at the bottom of the sheet.
3. Pick **Fast** (quick, short answers), **Normal** (the usual, marked **Recommended**) or **Deep** (thinks longer and
   checks its work twice; it takes more time and uses your limits faster).
- A level your plan does not include is locked and cannot be picked.

## How to change the model in voice mode
- In voice mode, tap the model pill at the bottom left. The same **Select model** sheet opens.

## Good to know
- Your choice is kept on this phone and used for every new message until you change it. You can change it in the
  middle of a chat.
- If a saved model is turned off by Classgrid or is no longer in your plan, the app goes back to **Auto**.
- If the sheet cannot check your plan, it says **Couldn't check which models your plan includes.** Tap **Retry**.
- When a plan limit for a model is reached, the chat shows a card with **See plans** or **Top up**.
