# Published pages and videos (phone app)

Classgrid AI can publish a web page (a link that starts with chat.classgrid.in/p/) or make a video for you. In the chat they
show as a card; on the phone they open in their own viewer, where you can comment, share, download and more.

## The card in the chat
- A page shows as a rounded card with an icon, the page's title and the word **Docs** under it.
- A video shows as a card with its first picture, "title · length" and the word **Docs** under it.
- Tap the card to open the page or the video.
- Pages and videos are also kept in Docs: side menu (tap ☰) then **Docs**. Rows are marked **Page** or **Video**;
  use the filter pill to show only **Pages** or **Videos**.

## How to open and use a page
1. Tap the page card in the chat, or open it from **Docs**.
2. The page works inside the app (buttons, forms and so on).
3. At the top: the share icon, the comments icon and ⋮ (**More**).
- ⋮ has **Pin** / **Unpin**, **Rename** (if you can edit), **Duplicate**, **Ask to comment** (if you cannot comment
  yet), **Copy link**, **Open in browser**, **Report page** (someone else's page) and **Delete** (owner).

## How to comment on a page
1. Open the page and tap the comments icon at the top.
2. Type in **Add a comment** and tap **Comment**.
3. Under a comment, tap **Reply** to answer it, or **Resolve** / **Reopen**.
4. Tap **Show resolved** to see resolved comments.

## How to open and play a video
1. Tap the video card in the chat, or open it from **Docs**.
2. Tap the video, or the play button, to play or pause.
3. Controls under the video: back to the start, **Loop** (on or off), play / pause and the download icon.
4. The row under them shows the time (for example 0:21 / 2:09), the sound button (sound on or off), **Comment** and
   the three dots, which hold **All comments** and the speed (**Half speed**, **Normal speed**, **1.5x speed**,
   **2x speed**).

## The scene timeline
- Under the controls is the scene strip: one tile per scene with its name and length. The scene playing now is
  outlined and a blue line shows where you are.
- Tap a tile to jump to the start of that scene.
- Comments show as dots (or a bar for a stretch of time) above the tiles.

## How to comment at a moment in a video
1. Open the video and tap **Comment** (shows only if you can comment).
2. Tap the video at the spot you mean: the comment is pinned at that time and point. Or drag on the video to draw a
   box. Or, on the scene strip, tap the start and then the end to pick a stretch of time.
3. Type in **Add a comment** and tap **Comment**. To stop, tap X on **Tap anywhere to comment**.
- Comments pinned at a time show as small cards under the video (text and time). Tap one to jump to that moment.
- To read all comments and reply, tap the comments icon at the top, or the three dots then **All comments**.

## How to share a page or a video
1. Open it and tap the share icon at the top.
2. Add people by email with a role (**Editor**, **Commenter** or **Viewer**) and tap **Share**.
3. Set **General access** to **Only people added** or **Anyone with the link**.
4. Tap **Copy link** or **Share link**. Approve or decline access requests at the top of the sheet.

## How to download a video
1. Open the video.
2. Tap the download icon under the video. Or tap the share icon, then the **Export** tab, then **Download MP4**.
3. The MP4 is saved in Downloads/Classgrid.

## Good to know
- Video ⋮ menu: **Pin** / **Unpin**, **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Comments**, **Ask to comment**, **Copy link**,
  **Open in browser**, **Report video**, **Delete** (owner).
- Deleting a page or video deletes it with its comments for everyone it is shared with. This cannot be undone.
- A page or video you have no access to shows **Request access**.
- Pages are not downloaded as files; use **Open in browser** or **Copy link**.
