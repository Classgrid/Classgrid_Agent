# What an answer can hold (phone app)

What can appear in a Classgrid AI answer in the Android app and how to use each part: code, diagrams, pictures,
tables, links, the AI's steps, follow-up questions and cards for pages, videos and files.

## How to see the steps of the AI's work
1. Under your question there is one line about the AI's work: while it works it says what it is doing (for example
   "Thinking"); when done it says something like **Worked through 3 steps** or **Thought**.
2. Tap that line. A sheet opens with every step, its thinking and what each step did. Swipe it down to close.
- When the AI uses helper agents, a chip like "2 agents" shows in the message box. Tap it to open the **Agent map**.

## How to use a code block
- The code card shows the language, the file name when there is one, and two buttons at the top right.
- Tap the copy icon (**Copy code**). It turns into a tick for a moment.
- Tap the expand icon (**Full screen**) to see the code on the whole screen with line numbers. There, tap the copy
  icon to copy, or tap ⋮ and **Wrap long lines** to turn wrapping on or off. Tap **X** to close.
- Long lines scroll sideways in the card.
- A ready-to-copy text (a prompt, an email, a message) shows in the same card with **Copy text** and full screen.

## How to use a diagram
- A diagram shows as a card titled **Diagram**. Tap **Code** to see its source, **Diagram** to go back.
- Tap ⋮ (**More**) for **Copy diagram** or **Copy source**.
- Tap the expand icon (**Full screen**). Pinch with two fingers to zoom and drag with one to move. Tap **X** to close.

## How to open a picture in an answer
1. Tap the picture. It opens full screen in the viewer, where you can zoom.
2. At the top: **Download** (saves it to the phone's Downloads, in a Classgrid folder), **Open in browser**, and ⋮
   with **Download**, **Open with another app**, **Open in browser**, **Copy link** and **Share**.
- Pictures the AI made for you work the same way (see the images page).

## Tables
- Tables have a header row and lines between cells. A table wider than the screen scrolls sideways.

## Links and sources
- Tap a link to open it. Classgrid links (docs, pages, shared chats, chats, calls, schedules) open inside the app;
  other links open in the phone's browser.
- A small pill after a sentence names the website it came from; tap it to open that page.
- When the answer used the web, **N sources** shows at the right under the answer. Tap it for the **Sources** list.

## Suggested follow-up questions
- Some answers end with suggested questions, one under the other. Tap one to send it as your next message.
- Some answers have buttons; tapping one sends its words as your next message.

## Cards for pages, videos and files
- A page the AI published shows as a card with its title and the word **Docs**. Tap it to open the page in the app.
- A video shows as a black box with a play button. Tap it to play in the app's player.
- A document the AI made (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV, text or zip) shows as a file card under the answer.
  Tap it to open it in the app's viewer.

## When the AI asks you something
- A card can open above the message box with the AI's questions. Tap an option, or type in **Something else**; tap
  **Skip** to skip one. Close it with **X**; tap the line in the answer to open it again.
- A plan card offers **Approve**; a command card offers **Run** or **Skip**.

## Good to know
- Answers can also hold charts, slides, maps, weather, clocks, countdowns, scores, timetables, maths and coloured
  notes. They are shown in the chat as they are.
- **Memory updated** under an answer means the AI saved something to your skills; tap it to open **Skills**.
- The row of icons under every answer (copy, like, reply, dislike, read aloud, retry) is on the chat basics page.
