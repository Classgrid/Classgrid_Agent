# Accounts: add, switch and remove (phone app)

The Classgrid phone app can keep up to 3 Classgrid accounts signed in at once, for example a personal and a work
account, and switch between them without signing in again.

## How to see your accounts
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap the gear (**Settings**).
2. Tap the account card at the top (your email, name and plan, with a small down arrow).
3. **Switch account** opens. Under **Accounts** every signed-in account is listed; the one in use has a check mark.

## How to add another account
1. Open **Settings** and tap the account card at the top.
2. Tap **Add account**.
3. The sign-in screen opens. Sign in with **Continue with Google**, **Continue with GitHub** or your email, the same
   as a normal sign-in (a new email can also create a new account here).
4. When the sign-in goes through, the new account is added and the app switches to it.
- To stop without adding, tap the back arrow at the top left (or Android's back) on the first sign-in step. Nothing
  is signed out.

## How to switch to another account
1. Open **Settings** and tap the account card at the top.
2. Tap the account you want.
3. "Switching to (email)" shows for a moment, then the app opens as that account.

## How to remove (sign out) one account
1. Open **Settings** and tap the account card at the top.
2. Tap the three dots (**⋮**) at the end of the account's row.
3. Tap **Sign out**.
- To sign out the account you are using now, use **Log out** at the bottom of Settings instead (see "Sign in and
  sign out (phone app)"). The app then switches to your next account.

## Good to know
- The limit is 3 accounts per phone. At 3, the **Add account** row is not shown: sign one out first.
- Each account has its own chats, settings, plan and Library. Nothing is shared between them.
- Notifications on this phone come for the account in use.
- If an account's sign-in has ended (for example it was logged out from another device), tapping it removes it from
  the list and opens the sign-in steps so you can sign in to it again.
- An account that still has sign-up steps to finish (WhatsApp, age, role) shows those steps when you switch to it.
