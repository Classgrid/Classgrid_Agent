# Voice mode and voice settings (phone app)

Voice mode lets you talk with Classgrid AI out loud and hear its answers, like a phone call. Voice settings is where
you pick the voice, the language you speak and check the microphone.

## How to start voice mode
1. In the chat, make sure the message box is empty.
2. Tap the round waves button at the right of the box (**Talk with Classgrid**).
3. The first time, Android asks to let Classgrid use the microphone: allow it.
4. The screen shows **Let's talk** with your name. It is already listening: just speak.

## How to talk
- Speak normally. When you stop for a moment, your words show in a grey bubble and the answer comes.
- While you speak a blue glow rises from the bottom. While Classgrid speaks a warm glow goes round the screen and each
  sentence lights up as it is said. After the answer it listens again by itself.
- Each finished answer has a copy icon under it.

## How to interrupt or pause
- While Classgrid speaks, tap the big button in the middle (**Stop and talk**). It stops and listens to you.
- While it listens, tap the big button (**Stop listening**) to switch the microphone off. The screen says
  **Tap the mic to talk**; tap the button again to talk.
- Tap the speaker button at the left of the big button to turn spoken answers off or on. With it off, answers show
  as text only.

## How to end voice mode
1. Tap the **X** at the right of the big button (**End voice chat**).
2. You are back in the chat. The talk is saved in that chat, with a **Voice chat ended** row and how long it lasted.

## What else is on the voice mode screen
- Top left: where the sound plays. Tap it for the phone's speaker, wired headphones or Bluetooth earbuds by name.
  Earbuds are used as soon as they connect. With none connected it says **No Bluetooth or headphones connected**.
- Top right: the gear opens **Language you speak**. Pick your language (for example English (India), Hindi, Marathi,
  Tamil, Spanish, Japanese).
- Bottom left: the model. Tap it to open **Select model**.
- Bottom right: the voice. Tap it to open **Voice** and pick a different voice.
- If your plan does not include voice or the model, a message shows with **Upgrade**.

## Voice mode in the background
- The talk goes on when you leave the app or lock the phone. A notification shows with **Hang up** and **Mute**.
  The notification needs Classgrid to be allowed to show notifications.

## How to open voice settings
1. Open the side menu (tap ☰ at the top left) and tap the gear at the bottom (Settings).
2. Tap **Voices** (it shows the voice now in use).

## What voice settings has
- **Permissions**: **Microphone**, **Voice mode listening**, **Phone speech recognition** (used by the mic button in the
  chat box) and **Speaker**, each saying if it is ready. If the microphone is not allowed, tap
  **Allow the microphone** or **Open phone settings**.
- **Test the microphone**: tap it and speak; it says whether it heard you.
- **Read answers aloud**: switch off to have voice mode answer in text only.
- **Language you speak**: the same list as in voice mode.
- **Voices**: slide the row of round balls, or tap one, to choose a voice and hear a sample. Tap **Hear it** to hear
  it again, or **Stop**.

## Good to know
- The voice you choose is one choice for the whole app: voice mode, voice settings and **Read aloud** under answers
  all use it.
- The speaker button in voice mode and **Read answers aloud** in voice settings are the same setting.
- Voice mode continues the chat that was open; from an empty new chat it starts a new chat.
- Voice mode uses the model you pick and counts toward your plan's limits.
